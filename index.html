About AIR Group

We are a group of researchers, at SUNY Binghamton. AIR group's research interests include Artificial Intelligence (AI), Robotics, and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). Our goal is to develop intelligent mobile robots that are able to interact with people, provide services to people, and learn from this experience, in human-inhabited, collaborative environments.

Student Award

December 2024

Eisuke Hirota received an Honorable Mentions in the Computing Research Association (CRA) Outstanding Undergraduate Research Award. CRA counts among its members more than 250 North American organizations active in computing research. Congratulations!

Student Award

May 2024

AIR alumni Yan Ding and Xiaohan Zhang received the Excellence in Research in Computer Science Award from SUNY Binghamton. Their dissertation topics are "Task and Motion Planning for Robots in Open Worlds" and "Symbol Grounding for Task and Motion Planning in Robotics" respectively. Congratulations!

Service Award

May 2024

PI Zhang received the Outstanding Associate Editor recognition from the IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) in 2024. The award was announced at the 2024 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) Award Ceremony on May 16 in Yokohama, Japan.

Feb 2024

David DeFazio and Eisuke Hirota developed a robotic guide dog for guiding people with visual impairment. This work was presented at The Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) in 2023. This research was covered by WIRED "Good bot! This robotic seeing-eye dog will guide the visually impaired".
